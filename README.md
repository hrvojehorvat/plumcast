# Plumcast

A "bring your own playlist" IPTV player for Android phones, Android TV and Amazon Fire TV.
Live TV with a TV guide, catch-up (replays), movies and series, built for the TV remote first.
No account, no tracking.

**Plum**, the voice assistant: open it from the menu (or tap the mic on a phone) and say what you want to watch, in English, German or Croatian. Plum searches live channels, replays from the TV guide and the provider's catch-up archive, films, series and categories; one clear match opens right away, several come as a short list to choose from. Speech is recognized by the device's own speech service; Plumcast itself sends nothing anywhere. Not available on Fire TV (no speech recognition for apps there).

**Download:** [plumcast.mrvoje.com](https://plumcast.mrvoje.com) or the [latest release](https://github.com/hrvojehorvat/plumcast/releases/latest) (`plumcast.apk`).

Plumcast is a player only: it ships no channels, playlists or content. Use it with a playlist you are entitled to (Xtream Codes login or an M3U link/file).
