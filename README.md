# Plumcast

A "bring your own playlist" IPTV player for Android phones, Android TV and Amazon Fire TV.
Live TV with a TV guide, catch-up (replays), movies and series, built for the TV remote first.
No account, no tracking.

**Plum**, the voice assistant: long-press OK on the remote (or tap the mic on a phone) and say what to watch, in English, German or Croatian, such as "MasterChef from two days ago", "pusti Nova TV" or "horror filmovi". Plum finds the replay, channel, movie, series or category and plays or opens it; when there are several, it lets you pick. Speech is recognized by the device's own speech service; Plum itself sends nothing anywhere. Not available on Fire TV (no speech recognition for apps there).

**Download:** [plumcast.mrvoje.com](https://plumcast.mrvoje.com) or the [latest release](https://github.com/hrvojehorvat/plumcast/releases/latest) (`plumcast.apk`).

Plumcast is a player only: it ships no channels, playlists or content. Use it with a playlist you are entitled to (Xtream Codes login or an M3U link/file).
